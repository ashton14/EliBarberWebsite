# Home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Conejo Valley men and dads on phones, deciding quickly. Primary action: Book (external booking link); secondary Call and Directions. Proof: real photos (coming), real Google reviews (TODO). One owner-barber.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop's black felt letterboard is the page system: facts (status, prices, durations, hours) are set as peg letters on ribbed felt, and photos are the promise. It refuses the moody vintage barbershop (pole stripes, script gold serif) and the white spa-salon.

OWN-WORLD: Ribbed black felt (#141312) and raised felt (#24221f), peg-white condensed caps (Barlow Condensed 600), receipt-paper light sections (#f3f1ec) with ink, and one gold (#d6a84e) used only for Book and the active state, with a bronze (#7d5f17) gold for text on paper. Board frames, leader dots, tabular prices. Atkinson Hyperlegible body.

STORY: A visitor sees status and hours, sees a fresh cut, sees what it costs and how long it takes, trusts a real person, and books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo; a framed felt letterboard sits over its lower edge carrying OPEN NOW / CLOSES status, the H1, a one-line value prop, a gold Book button and a Call button. A sticky bottom bar (Book | Call | Directions) on phones.

FORM: grounded candidate 4 of 7 (shop letterboard price menu), user-amended to black and gold. seed:27d9ee8f3b854a39. Raises: collider (prices pinned to items in tabular figures), black hole (gold reserved for the action), seed packet (photo = promise, board line = facts), brine calendar (status in plain words).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
